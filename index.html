How it works

Architecture & stack

A lightweight, low-cost stack that runs on the tools the plant already has.

01 · CAPTURE

Shop-floor entry

Operators enter OEE, QC and 5S data through simple web forms on any device.

02 · STORE

Central database

Google Sheets acts as the structured database with history and validation.

03 · AUTOMATE

Logic layer

Google Apps Script handles lookups, calculations, workflow and alerts.

04 · REPORT

Dashboards

Real-time dashboards and MIS views for the floor and management.
